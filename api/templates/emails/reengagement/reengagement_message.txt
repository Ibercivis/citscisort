What is CitSciSort? We are reading the scientific literature about citizen science and sorting it: is each paper reporting scientific findings, or is it research *about* citizen science itself (its methods, participants, data quality, impact...)? Every classification you make helps build the first map of this field.

Each abstract takes about two minutes. You can do one or twenty, whenever you want.
