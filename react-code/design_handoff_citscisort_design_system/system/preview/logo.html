Brand

Logo — sorting citizen-science papers

The mark depicts the product's core action: an incoming paper is sorted into one of two bins — Science (filled, findings) on the left, and Meta-science (outlined, a paper-about-papers) on the right. A single source forks into two paths, matching the first decision every classifier makes in the Classify flow.

Note. The source repo ships Vite's default starter logo as its favicon — not a CitSci brand mark. The real "logo" in the live product is the text <Typography variant="h6" color="primary">CitSci Sort</Typography> in the sidebar. The mark below is a proposal introduced by this design system, grounded in the product's sorting metaphor.

The idea
Incoming abstract forks into…
Science (filled)
Findings — original research output. Solid/filled bin echoes "substance".
Meta-science (outlined)
Research about research — a nested paper inside the bin makes the meta relation explicit.

Wordmark · lockup
On brand primary (reverse)
Place the mark on a white tile when appearing on primary surfaces — the mark itself is designed to sit on primary blue, so a nested reversal is the cleanest composition.

Clear space & don'ts
  • Minimum clear space = the height of one bin on the mark (~16px at 48px size).
  • Minimum size: 24px square. Below that, swap to the solid blue tile with a single white "C" monogram.
  • Don't swap the bin treatments — the filled left / outlined right pairing encodes the taxonomy.
  • Don't rotate, skew, or recolor the mark except to reverse (white on primary).
  • Don't combine the mark with other logos into a lockup without sign-off.
